Support

Found a wrong translation, a bug, or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub.

I don't hear any audio

The app reads Hebrew with your phone's built-in voice.

  • iPhone: turn up the volume. If it's still silent, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Hebrew and download a voice.
  • Android: go to Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech, and make sure your speech engine has Hebrew installed.

The audio is too fast

In the app, open Settings (the gear icon) and lower Speed. You can go down to 0.4×.

How do levels work?

Each level is 50 words, ordered by how often people say them. Level 1 is the 50 most common words. Nothing is locked: tap Change on the home screen to jump to any level.

What does "solid" mean?

A word counts as solid once you've got it right twice in a row in study or quiz. Miss it and it starts over, and it comes back first the next time you study.

Can I hide the vowels?

Yes. In Settings, turn off Show nikud.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress. This erases every answer and your level from the device, and it can't be undone.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Progress is stored on the device, with no account. It comes along if you restore your phone from an iCloud or Google backup, but it doesn't sync between devices.
